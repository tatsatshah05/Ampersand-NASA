SPHEREx motion viewer

Each dot is one SPHEREx exposure. Every exposure sees the sky through a different colour of infrared light, so together they show how bright the object is at each wavelength: its spectrum. The brightness is added up inside the dashed circle on the image (15″ in radius), so anything else inside that circle is included.

How this is measured

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a star to see its spectrum, space = play/pause, arrow keys = step. North is up, east is left. Images: NASA/JPL-Caltech SPHEREx, from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA). Star positions: ESA Gaia.
